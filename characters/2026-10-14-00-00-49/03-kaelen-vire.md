# Kaelen Vire

**Age:** 24  
**Appearance:** Kaelen has a lean, wiry frame, with sun-kissed skin that hints at years spent on the water. His dark hair is cropped short, and his eyes—sharp and amber-brown—hold the quiet resolve of someone who’s never been afraid to fight for what little he has. A faint scar runs from his temple to his cheek, a reminder of a past that still lingers in the corners of his mind.

**One-line hook:** A man of the sea and the streets, Kaelen Vire fights for what little he has left.

**Personality:** Kaelen is pragmatic and driven, with a quiet intensity that masks a deep well of resilience. Though he often keeps his emotions guarded, he has a fierce loyalty to those who stand by him. He is clever and resourceful, but his past has left him wary of trust, especially when it comes to family.

**Backstory:** Born on the coast, Kaelen grew up fishing with his brothers and sisters, learning the rhythms of the sea from a young age. But when his parents left for a new life in the city, he was left behind—estranged and alone in a small town that never felt like home. He spent years working odd jobs, scraping by, but his sharp mind and keen instincts led him to a life of crime. Through an apprenticeship in the city’s underworld, he learned how to survive—and how to fight. Now, with a doubled career length and a past marked by neglect, he’s determined to carve out a future for himself, even if it means leaving the past behind.

**Strengths and flaws:** His **Cunning** is his greatest strength, allowing him to outsmart opponents and navigate the murky waters of crime with ease. His **Endurance** and **Dexterity** also give him a solid edge in both physical and mental challenges. However, his **Charisma** and **Perception** are his weakest links, making it harder for him to read people and connect with others on a deeper level.

**Motivation:** Kaelen wants to rebuild what was lost—not just his family’s connection, but the home he once knew. He fights not just for survival, but for the chance to reclaim a life that was taken from him.

**Story seed:** When a long-lost relative resurfaces with a message from their estranged parents, Kaelen is forced to confront the past he’s tried so hard to leave behind—while a new crime syndicate begins to take notice of his growing influence.
