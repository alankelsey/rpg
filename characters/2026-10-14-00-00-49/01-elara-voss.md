# Elara Voss

**Age:** 22  
**Appearance:** Elara has a lean, agile frame with a faint scar running from her left eyebrow to her cheekbone, a remnant of a childhood fall. Her dark hair is often tied in a loose braid, and her eyes—sharp and amber-brown—seem to hold the weight of quiet observation. She wears a faded military academy jacket over a simple gray turtleneck, and her hands are always slightly calloused from years of moving between places.

**One-line hook:** A quiet observer with a mind sharpened by hardship and a past she hasn’t yet fully unraveled.

**Personality:** Calm and thoughtful, Elara is the kind of person who listens more than she speaks. She has a sharp mind and a keen eye for detail, but she also carries the weight of unspoken expectations. Though she may seem distant at times, she is deeply loyal to those she trusts.

**Backstory:** Born to a middle-class family on the frontier, Elara grew up in the suburbs of a once-thriving town that had since fallen into decline. Her parents were both hardworking, but her mother’s chronic illness and her father’s constant need to find new work created a quiet pressure in the household. Elara was the youngest of three, and while her siblings had their own dreams, she found herself drawn to the military academy, where she could escape the constant uncertainty of home. She excelled there, but never truly left the weight of her past behind. Now, as a drifter moving between places, she carries the memory of a childhood shaped by hardship and the lingering question of why her mother’s illness never seemed to improve.

**Strengths and flaws:**  
**Highest stats:** Intelligence (11.4), Charisma (10.7), Luck (10.7)  
**Lowest stats:** Cunning (9.3), Mental Fortitude (9.1)  

**Motivation:** Elara seeks meaning in the quiet moments—between places, between people. She wants to understand the world not just as it is, but as it could be, and she carries the hope that one day she’ll be able to make sense of the past that still lingers in her steps.

**Story seed:** While traveling through a forgotten town on the edge of the frontier, Elara recognizes a place from her childhood. As she walks its familiar streets, she begins to question whether the past she thought she left behind is still waiting for her.
