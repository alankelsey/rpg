# Soren Lir

**Age:** 24  
**Appearance:** Soren has a lean, athletic build with a faint scar running from his left eyebrow to his cheekbone, a remnant of a childhood accident. His dark hair is usually tousled, and his eyes — a sharp, stormy gray — seem to take in everything around him. He wears a faded leather jacket over a plain white shirt, with a small silver pendant hanging from his neck.

**One-line hook:** A former child star with a talent for survival, Soren Lir walks the line between memory and escape.

**Personality:** Soren is observant and adaptable, with a quiet confidence that masks a deep need for control. He’s driven by a desire to escape his past, yet he can’t quite let go of the life he once had. His sharp wit and keen intuition make him a compelling presence, but his struggles with addiction and a lingering sense of abandonment often leave him feeling untethered.

**Backstory:** Born into a showbiz family in a dense city, Soren was raised in the countryside by a single parent who died before he was ten. His famous father was a household name, and Soren grew up with the advantages of elite schooling, but also the weight of expectation. After his parent’s death, he was left to navigate the limelight alone, and by the time he turned sixteen, he had already been in the industry for nearly a decade. His career took off with a lucky break that came with a ×1.5 multiplier, but the pressure came with it — and when he was finally free to leave the industry, he found himself struggling with a mild addiction and the feeling that someone had been left behind.

**Strengths and flaws:**  
**Strengths:**  
- **Charisma (11.5):** Soren’s natural charm and stage presence make him a compelling figure, both in and out of the spotlight.  
- **Perception (10.8):** His keen awareness of people and situations helps him navigate both personal and professional challenges.  

**Flaws:**

**Motivation:** Soren wants to be free from the expectations of his past, but he also longs for a sense of belonging — something he’s never quite managed to find.

**Story seed:** Soren is offered a chance to return to the spotlight, but he must choose between the life he once had and the life he’s trying to build — a decision complicated by the reappearance of someone from his past.
