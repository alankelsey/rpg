# Taryn Vey

**Age:** 32  
**Appearance:** Taryn has a lean, battle-worn frame with a scar running from her temple to her jawline, a relic from a past ambush. Her dark hair is cropped short, streaked with silver at the temples, and her eyes—sharp and amber-brown—hold the weight of years spent fighting and surviving. She wears a faded leather jacket over a plain grey shirt, and her hands are calloused from years of handling weapons and dealing in shadow.

**One-line hook:** A battle-scarred survivor, haunted by the past and driven by the need to escape it.

**Personality:** Taryn is pragmatic, hardened by years of conflict and crime. She speaks little, but when she does, her words carry weight. She's fiercely loyal to those she trusts, but her empathy is limited by the trauma that lingers in her bones. She carries herself with quiet confidence, though the ghosts of her past often whisper in her ear.

**Backstory:** Born in a conflict zone, Taryn grew up watching war tear through her family and her home. Her mother died when she was a child, caught in the crossfire of a battle that left her father missing for years. She was raised in an immigrant enclave, where survival meant working hard and staying quiet. She never had the luxury of schooling, but she learned the value of discipline and the cost of failure. By the time she was twenty, she had already fought in two wars and had a reputation as a soldier who never backed down. After returning from the last war, she fell into crime, using her skills and knowledge of battle to carve out a life in the shadows. But addiction took hold of her, and it's a battle she's still fighting—especially now that someone from her past has reappeared, pulling her back into a world she thought she'd left behind.

**Strengths and flaws:**  
- **Strength:** 11.5 (+15%) — She's strong enough to take down enemies in hand-to-hand combat.  
- **Empathy:** 8.7 (-13%) — Her trauma has made it hard for her to connect with others, especially those who remind her of her past.

**Motivation:** Taryn wants to escape the past that haunts her, but she also wants to protect the person who still believes in her. She fights not just for survival, but for redemption.

**Story seed:** A letter arrives from the person she left behind, and the past she thought was buried begins to rise again.
