# Rhea Dain

**Age:** 28  
**Appearance:** Rhea has a lean, wiry frame, her skin a deep bronze from years of working under the desert sun. Her hair is cropped short and streaked with silver, a testament to her hard life. Her eyes, sharp and amber, seem to hold the weight of many years, but her lips often curl into a wry smile that softens her fierce look.

A wiry survivor with a silver-streaked smile and the weight of years behind her eyes.

**Personality**  
Rhea is a blend of quiet resilience and sharp wit. She has a deep well of patience, honed by years of hardship, but she also knows when to laugh at life’s absurdities. Her humor is often dry, but it hides a fierce determination. She is observant and intuitive, able to read people and situations with ease, though she sometimes lets her past burdens cloud her judgment.

**Backstory**  
Born in the harsh desert, Rhea spent her early years struggling with a chronic illness that left her weaker than most. Her parent died when she was still a child, and she grew up in the inner city, scraping by with whatever work she could find. She was sent to a military academy, where she learned discipline and the value of hard work. After years of training and manual labor, she became a skilled tradesperson, her career lengthened by a multiplier that reflects her dedication. Yet, even with her success, poverty still lingers in the shadows, and a secret from her past remains unresolved.

**Strengths and Flaws**  
**Strengths:**  
- **Mental Fortitude (12.1):** Rhea’s unshakable resolve has helped her endure hardship and illness, allowing her to push through even the toughest challenges.  
- **Cunning (10.8):** Her ability to think quickly and adapt has made her a skilled worker and a perceptive observer of people and situations.  

**Flaws:**  
- **Strength (9.1):** Though she is physically capable, her endurance is not as high as her mental fortitude, and she sometimes struggles with the physical demands of her work.  
- **Luck (8.8):** Rhea often feels as though the odds are stacked against her, and she has a tendency to expect the worst, which can sometimes slow her down.

**Motivation**  
Rhea is driven by the desire to secure a better future—not just for herself, but for those she cares about. She wants to break free from the cycle of hardship that has defined much of her life, and she is determined to keep her secret from the past from coming back to haunt her.

**Story Seed**  
When a long-lost relative arrives with a message from her parent’s past, Rhea is forced to confront a secret that has been buried for years—one that could change everything she thought she knew.
